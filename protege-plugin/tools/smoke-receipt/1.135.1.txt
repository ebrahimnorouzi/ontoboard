WHAT THIS RELEASE ADDS

- An imported pattern is drawn as its hierarchy, a class above its subclasses, in more columns
  when there are many. A class two patterns share is drawn once, in the frame of the pattern that
  came first, and the other frame says where it is. A superclass that no pattern lists stands in
  its subclass's frame, above it.
- Lines are routed round the boxes, at right angles, 12 px apart at least, and no two share a
  stretch; labels are placed clear of boxes, other labels and other lines. Measured on the study's
  board: 0 px of lines run together, where 1,280 px did; a board of 120 boxes is routed in full.
- Every pattern term and imported term is shown by its label, with its identifier under it, in
  the board, the import dialogs, the term lists and the quality report.
- Right-click on the board opens its menu on macOS and Linux. Extending an imported pattern works:
  its classes and properties are offered when relating terms and setting parents.
- The board arranges itself when a term or pattern arrives, until somebody moves a box. Frames
  keep their rows. Dragging inside a frame moves the board. Property sections go below the classes
  when that gives the board a screen's shape. Disjointness, ranges and rule lines wait until a
  term is selected, and so do domains but for a property beside its class; class expressions
  are off by default.
- A property whose domain names one class on the board stands beside that class, in its frame,
  joined to it by its domain line, which is drawn always; its range waits for a selection, and so
  does a line between two properties that stand apart. Found by the maintainer on the pizza
  ontologies, where every property stood in a frame of its own, joined to nothing until clicked.
  A board saved by an earlier build keeps its places until Arrange > Outline, which now says
  "classes as a tree, properties by their class".
- Definition... on a term's menu. Release asks Save first?. A test can be replaced. The pattern
  library searches the labels of a pattern's terms, says when a pattern is already imported, and
  names the files it writes. Escape closes the import dialogs.
- The small issues testers had left open: a range's datatype stands under its property, not alone
  in a corner; adding a subclass moves only the subclasses after it; a class found and added, or
  brought by an import, stands under its superclass in that superclass's frame; frames only for
  patterns the ontology imports; an import taken back is said to be gone; 25 bundled patterns carry
  IAO's current labels and definitions; Ethnicgroup's IRIs; released identifiers read when the
  project opens; lines keep clear of arrowheads and labels off frame outlines; "another version of"
  for patterns sharing an IRI; a badge on defined classes; HermiT asked after ELK, with a warning;
  labels made from names for a pattern's unlabelled terms.
- Fixed: re-importing left Protege holding a detached module; Save and build raised a false
  "changed outside of Protege"; a test on a deleted class failed for ever; Definition... deleted a
  term's other definitions; released identifiers could be minted again; release notes covered
  classes only; a garbled Base IRI was accepted. docs/fixed.md has each with how it was found.
- Found on Windows and fixed: the board drew its logic symbols as empty boxes (Segoe UI has no
  "∃ ∀ ⊔ ⊓ ∧ ▸", and Java gives a named font no fallback there; labels now draw with the fallback
  behind Segoe UI, every other glyph pixel for pixel as before); the empty board's help lines were
  near white (they took the theme's colour for disabled text); switching the board's arrangement
  emptied the patterns' frames; taking a term out of an import broke the next build; a class with
  no members sent everybody to Undo, where the build now names the parent or arrow that empties
  it; the build's advice, passing tests and first warning; Find's Enter, count and zoom; the
  relation dialog names its property, says when a relation does not fit, and shows the new arrow;
  "Saved." after an Undo; New ODK project's squeezed refusal; an undone import's traces; Airline
  and Airline (OWL) named after a path on a disk; a new project's pages, README and .gitignore.
- Properties on the board: a property stated to be its own sub-property no longer draws a loop on
  its box; two properties that are each other's inverse stand side by side, their line short and
  inside the frame; and the domains, ranges and disjointness a selected term shows are routed round
  the boxes, among the lines already drawn, which keep their routes; an image exported in the
  moment after a click has all of them.
- Found by reviews of these fixes, and fixed: a sticky note resized by its handles went back to
  its old size at the next redraw; a long data value lay on the next column of individuals. And
  by CI's Linux build: there DejaVu Sans makes property boxes one line tall, and an inverse
  partner could stand on a sub-property's left, where their line went over the top of the box.
- The documentation site draws its pattern graphs with ontoink 0.8.0, and Ethnicgroup's example
  names the pattern's own IRIs, not a path on the machine it was harvested on - the site's check
  that every example matches its pattern failed on it.


WHAT THIS RECEIPT DOES NOT COVER

- The scripted runs send mouse events to the components directly, so focus, hover, drag and the
  native file dialogs were exercised only in the guide's first two tasks, walked once with a real
  mouse and keyboard on Windows.
- Linux: not run. The right-click fix covers it by the same code path as macOS.
- A real window activation for the Save and build fix: emulated in Protege by calling its own
  handler at the moment the OS would; one check by hand with Protege in front closes it.
- Old boards: a board saved by 1.135.0 keeps the frames of patterns it no longer imports, empty,
  and they push the outline down; a class defined by an equivalence stands apart, below the
  frames, its lines running up the board. Seen on pizza; not fixed in this release.
