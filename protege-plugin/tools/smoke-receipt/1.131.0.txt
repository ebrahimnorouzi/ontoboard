WHAT THIS RELEASE ADDS

- The board shows the whole ontology. Opened on an ontology with nothing saved, it is filled
  and arranged as an outline: design patterns framed along the top, classes as a tree read left
  to right with each class's individuals and restrictions beside it, object and data properties
  in frames of their own. A term made anywhere in Protege appears beside its parent; Hide from
  board is remembered; More > Show every term replaces Add all and Add selected, which are gone.
  Opening a board writes no file until the board is changed. A board saved by an earlier version
  keeps what it shows, and says so. Up to 1,500 terms, the ontology's own first.
- Focus: selecting a term brings it and everything it is connected to forward and fades the
  rest. On a board of more than 150 relations, disjointness, domains and ranges are drawn for the
  term in focus, and other relations faintly until one of their ends is selected.
- Domains and ranges are drawn, from the property's node; so are data property domains and
  ranges, equivalent properties, a property equivalent to an inverse, disjointness with a
  restriction, an equivalence between two restrictions and a functional data property.
- Arrange > Outline. Boxes fit their labels. Lines are painted behind boxes, on screen and in an
  exported image. Restriction and datatype nodes keep their place.
- An edge between two frames goes around any frame in between.
- Find terms: an ontology one of whose imports cannot be fetched loads, and names the import;
  so do Import terms and Refresh imports. A term the ontology only mentions shows where it is
  from and comes after its own.
- Edits made while the board is not on screen redraw it when it is shown, once.
