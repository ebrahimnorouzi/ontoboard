OntoBoard 1.139.0 - host smoke receipt

The assistant can now suggest OWL as well as annotations and removals. Each change has a test
that failed first:
- add-axiom and replace-axiom proposals in Manchester syntax, read with the OWL API's parser
  over the ontology's own names. An unknown name is reported, never declared.
- "Write as OWL..." asks for a sentence about the term. "Suggest a better axiom" sits on a
  semantics line.
- The reasoner checks an addition or replacement on a copy before Accept, and says which
  classes become unsatisfiable. An "only" with no "some" beside it gets a note.
- A justification that puts a typed value against a range of another value space says so:
  in OWL 2, xsd:decimal and xsd:float have disjoint value spaces.
- What is sent quotes a name with a space, so that what the model copies parses back.
- The fixes for the 1.138.0 review's Important findings: HermiT accepts xsd:date again, the
  60 s limit bounds the whole check, Stop interrupts it, "(SEM)" is not read as a language, a
  card never stays "Checking...", and accepting a label leaves the same answer's definition
  card good.

Produced on Windows 11 Pro (10.0.26100), with tools/smoke.ps1 and -Dontoboard.selftest=true. The
verdict was read from what Protege logged. Protege 5.6.9 is the maintainer's own install, with
ontoboard-1.138.0.jar moved to ~/Documents/ontoboard-jar-backup and this jar the only
ontoboard-*.jar in plugins; Protege 5.5.0 is the throwaway copy, unblocked (see 1.138.0). Each

- The datatype-clash fixture is now repaired in one round.

- The pizza case is the instructive failure. The model now names the clash correctly ("xsd:decimal
  and xsd:float have disjoint value spaces") and proposes the right repair: replace

  answer solves the pizza case in round 1.

A fresh-context review of 1.139.0 found no Critical issue and four Important ones, fixed in
1.140.0 with a test each:
- A card showed the model's text, but Accept wrote the parsed axiom, and the two could differ.
  Text after a data range was dropped, a term named twice became "DisjointWith owl:Thing", and
  a synonym or "Thing" resolved to a term the card did not name.
- Accepting one card of an answer, such as a label, made the same answer's axiom cards invalid.
- Revise on an axiom card sent "Revise your proposal for null null".
- HermiT can lose a single interrupt between two tableau tests, so neither the 60 s limit nor
  Stop reliably ended a check.
A fifth, re-graded to Important: each check's ontology copy stayed reachable for up to 60 s.
This version is therefore not one to release; 1.140.0 is its successor.
