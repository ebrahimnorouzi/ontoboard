OntoBoard 1.140.0 - host smoke receipt

The assistant benchmark measures text to axiom, and the 1.139.0 review's findings are fixed.
Each change has a test that failed first:
- text-to-axiom: an axiom of a class is held back and said as a sentence. HermiT judges the
  model's axiom: does it say at least that, without making a class empty? Cases come from
  NFDIcore 3.0.4 and MWO 3.0.0 (snapshots, CC0), pizza-bfo and the fixtures. NFDIcore and MWO
  define their classes with EquivalentTo, so a definition becomes a sentence that states both
  directions, and only an answer that states both passes.
- A card shows the axiom as Accept writes it, under the ontology's names. Text after a data
  range, a term named twice, and a class labelled "Thing" are refused rather than rewritten.
- Accepting one card leaves the same answer's axiom cards valid, and Revise on an axiom card
  names the axiom.
- A reasoner check ends within its minute, or on Stop, even when HermiT loses an interrupt or a
  reasoner ignores it; a check's ontology copy is let go at once.
- A name with a space, written without its quotes, is read; the card shows what was read.

Of the ten failures:
- Five name a property that does not exist (hasReinforcement for 'reinforced with', hasTopping
  for 'has topping'). OntoBoard refuses them and declares nothing. The model was shown only the
  subject's block, so nothing told it that 'reinforced with' is a property. 1.141.0 sends the
  terms the sentence names.
- Four are not Manchester syntax ("And", "some" before the property).
- One writes SubClassOf for a sentence that states both directions. It is valid, but it says
  half of what was asked.
No answer invented an IRI.

The 1.139.0 repair answers were scored again offline with this jar's code. The pizza case is now
solved in round 1: the model had written 'has value' without its quotes. The other recorded
repairs score as before.

A fresh-context review of 1.140.0 found no Critical issue. It confirmed that each case's
request is byte-identical to what "Write as OWL..." sends, and that 300 Stop races ended without
a hang. It found three Important issues and, by their effect, three of its Minor ones were
raised to Important. All are fixed in 1.141.0 with a test each:
- An "only" answer passed where "some" was held back whenever the rest of the ontology made it
  imply "some". A case now passes only on an answer equivalent to the held-back axiom.
- One answer the reasoner could not handle, such as an unsupported facet, ended a whole run, and
  every recorded answer was lost.
- The request told the model to ask for missing terms, but the terms the sentence names were not
  in the context. 1.141.0 sends them, and an answer that asks is reported as such.
- Putting quotes back around names could change or refuse an axiom that was valid as written.
- The second axiom of an add-axiom ("with:") was dropped silently.
- An API key cleaned of invisible characters could escape redaction in the benchmark's output.
This version is therefore not one to release; 1.141.0 is its successor.
