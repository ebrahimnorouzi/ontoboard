FIXED IN 1.134.1

- A sheet narrower than the window was cut off part of the way across. Reported from Protege
  5.6.9 with 1.134.0 on pizza-bfo's four-column sheets: the two header rows ran across the whole
  tab, while the rows beneath them stopped inside the second column, the rest grey - Name and
  Role of could not be seen. The panel above the grid was a plain JPanel, which its viewport
  stretches to the tab's width; the header-row table in it shares the grid's columns and spread
  that width across them, while the grid, which does not resize its columns, stayed as wide as
  they had asked to be - and drew them clipped at its own edge. The panel is now a Scrollable
  that keeps its own width, so both tables lay the columns out alike, at the widths their
  contents asked for. SheetGridWidthTest: 480 px of grid under 1,945 px of columns before, all
  four columns after.
