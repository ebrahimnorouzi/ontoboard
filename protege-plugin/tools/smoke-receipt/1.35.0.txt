ROBOT > Transform... > Materialize relations

Where "Reason" asserts the subclass axioms a reasoner infers, materialize asserts the existential
relations it infers - that a pizza with a mozzarella topping has a topping that is a cheese
topping. A plain reasoner leaves that implicit and a downstream consumer cannot work it out, which
is why OBO release pipelines run it.

It is offered over every object property in the imports closure, not over an empty set. ROBOT's
CLI selects properties with --term, and handing the operation an empty selection selects nothing -
the operation would run an expensive reasoner and then report "no change" on an ontology it could
have said plenty about. RobotTransformTest asserts it adds axioms and removes none, because an
operation that changes nothing is indistinguishable from one that is broken.

MaterializeOperation was checked with javap -v over its whole constant pool - no RDF4J, no
openrdf, no POI - which is the check that matters after ReportOperation and QueryOperation both
turned out to be unusable in a bundle for reasons no method signature revealed.

Nothing was needed in TransformAction: it builds its dropdown from Kind.values(), so the new
operation and its help text appear on their own. That is the design paying off rather than a
change worth claiming.
