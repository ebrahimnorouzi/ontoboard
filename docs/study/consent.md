## What this is

We are measuring how long some ordinary ontology-curation work takes, how accurately it is done,
and how it feels to do - with two sets of tools. You will do two comparable pieces of work, one
with each, and fill in three short questionnaires. It takes **80 minutes**.

**We are measuring the tools, not you.** There is no score you can fail, and nothing you do will
be attributed to you. If a task turns out to be impossible in the time, that is a finding about
the tool, which is the point.

## What you will be asked to do

1. Answer six background questions (experience, which tools you use).
2. Ten minutes of training, five on each tool.
3. Two 20-minute tasks: repair a spreadsheet of ontology terms, add three new terms, and run some
   checks. One task with each tool. Each task is capped at 20 minutes and stopping at the cap is
   expected and normal.
4. A 10-minute task in which somebody else edits the same ontology at the same time.
5. Three questionnaires (about 4 minutes in total) and a short conversation at the end.

We will ask you to **think aloud** while you work. This feels strange for the first minute and
then stops feeling strange.

## What is recorded

| Recorded | Not recorded |
|---|---|
| The screen of the machine you are working on | Video of you |
| Your voice, while you work and in the debrief | Your name, anywhere in the data |
| The files you edit | Your keystrokes outside the task windows |
| Times, counts of defects found, questionnaire answers | Anything from your own accounts - you will not be asked to sign in to anything |

You are given a participant number at the start. **Your name appears only on this signed sheet**,
which is kept separately from the data, and the data carries only the number. The list linking
names to numbers is destroyed once the analysis is finished; after that the data cannot be traced
back to you by us or by anybody else.

## Your rights

Taking part is voluntary. You may stop at any moment, without giving a reason, and nothing follows
from it. If you stop, say whether you want the data collected so far deleted or kept - either is
fine.

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask what data about you we hold (Art. 15), to have it
corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17), to restrict its processing (Art. 18), to receive a copy
(Art. 20) and to withdraw this consent at any time (Art. 7(3)) without affecting the lawfulness of
what was processed before. You may also complain to the supervisory authority. **Once the
name-to-number list is destroyed we can no longer find your data to delete it**, so a request to
delete has to reach us before then - the date is above.
